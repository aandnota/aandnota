Small Deaths

Animations made between 2021 - 2026

Part of an exhibition at NP3, 2025

Within the Terrors of Thought, Various Everyday Behaviours, and Not in My Backyard form a three-part series of 3D animated loops, called Small Deaths, in which I confront death, grief, and displacement through a recurring figure — a stand-in for myself — that migrates across each piece's landscape. I build all three in Blender from a mix of photoscanned and hand-modeled forms, and I deliberately retain the software's own wireframe UI elements in the final render, exposing the scaffolding of my own construction: an attempt to diagram and measure experiences — vertigo, ritual, displacement — that resist being measured at all.

Each piece isolates a different register of loss for me. In Within the Terrors of Thought, I suspend the figure atop a cratered stone in a dizzying vertical orbit, rendering grief as disorientation rather than narrative. In Various Everyday Behaviours, I stage a ritual cycle of ascent and descent, a body dismembered and carried in procession as flowers bloom and recede — the sublime, inarticulate fact of being alive set against the mundane, and against the certainty that I will die. In Not in My Backyard, I move the figure through a scorched, wind-scoured landscape littered with the debris of an invented religion, carrying the last living thing between an above-world and an underworld beneath a wrecked satellite dish — displacement with nowhere left to land. Together, the three pieces trace a single body through mourning, ritual, and exile.

Each work is a seamless loop lasting close to three minutes, for a combined runtime of just over six minutes. I conceived, animated, and sound-designed all three alone, with no collaborators. I score each with only ambient/environmental sound — wind, fabric, fire, forest and animal noise, tree limbs, microphone feedback — rather than music, keeping the works quiet and immersive rather than directive.
