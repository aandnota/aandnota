Alex Myers is Associate Professor of Interaction Design and Director of Graphic Design & Film in the Department of Computer Science, Design, and Journalism at Creighton University. Often interrogating issues of immersion, affect, violence, and interactivity, Alex's new media work has been widely exhibited in international venues across North America and Europe. His work has also received numerous grants and awards, including the Provincie Groningen Kunst en Cultuur Prijs in 2009 and 2014; the Electric Objects Artist Grant in 2016; and the “Best Interactive” Award from the Austin Music Video Festival in 2017. In his work, Alex interrogates the critical roles which technologies familiar from mainstream media contexts can play in experimental art practices.

I connect disparate thoughts and create surrealistic scenes by exploring the potentials of material transmutation. Surrendering to digital cannibalism, the body of my work becomes an interrogation of the violence that immersion, affect, and interactivity inflict on a self marked by grief, mortality, and displacement. Utilizing the processes and tools of mainstream digital entertainment — and leaving their own scaffolding exposed — I reconfigure these experiences into works that mourn, ritualize, and refuse to resolve.
